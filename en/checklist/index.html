Hanoi Motorbike Rental Checklist: What to Check Before Pickup

This checklist prioritises safety and clear rental terms. Keep a written record of what you agreed to, especially if the trip involves multiple days.

01

Before booking

Choose a vehicle you can legally and confidently operate. Confirm availability, delivery location, total quoted price and what is included. Ask for cancellation, return and maintenance arrangements in writing.

02

Before signing

Read the duration, deposit, identification requirements, insurance clauses and damage responsibilities. Clarify who responds to a breakdown, what counts as late return and how the deposit is refunded.

03

At handover

Check tyres, front and rear brakes, lights, indicators, horn, mirrors and locking system. Record mileage and fuel where relevant. Photograph existing damage and test the bike safely before accepting it.

04

Before riding

Verify current licensing and vehicle rules with an official source. Wear a suitable helmet, plan realistic distances, check the weather and never ride when tired or otherwise unfit.

05

When returning

Follow the agreed time and location. Document the bike’s condition, settle itemised charges, retain receipts and request written confirmation of the deposit settlement.

General guidance only. Confirm current rules and terms with official sources and the rental provider.
