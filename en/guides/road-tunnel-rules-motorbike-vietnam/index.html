Riding Through Road Tunnels in Vietnam

The rules for riding a motorbike through road tunnels in Vietnam: dipped headlights, no stopping, overtaking, U-turns or reversing, what to do if you break down inside, and the fines.

On longer trips through Vietnam the road regularly dives into a tunnel, whether under a mountain pass or as an underpass in the city. For a motorbike rider, going from bright daylight into half-darkness is a moment when a lot happens at once: your eyes have to adjust, the noise gets louder and there is hardly any room to swerve. That is why the Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety has dedicated tunnel rules. This guide explains them, lists the amounts set by Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP and says what to do if you break down inside. For an overview of riding safely, see our topic page on safer riding and breakdown support.

How tunnels are signposted

A tunnel ahead is announced by warning sign W.240 "Đường hầm" in the road sign regulation QCVN 41:2024/BGTVT. Under Article 12(3)(đ) of the law, riders must pay attention and slow down or stop where safety requires at underpasses and road tunnels, so ease off the throttle before the entrance.

Rule 1: dipped headlights on

Article 26(1) requires motor vehicles to use dipped headlights in a tunnel; non-motorised vehicles need a light or a reflector. Many newer scooters ride with the headlight permanently on, but check your rental bike before you set off. Riding through a tunnel without dipped headlights costs 600,000 to 800,000 VND under Article 7(3)(h) of Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP.

Rule 2: no stopping, no parking

Nobody stops in a tunnel, not even briefly for a photo. Article 26(2) bans stopping and parking in tunnels. Stopping or parking there outside the places provided for it costs 800,000 to 1,000,000 VND under Article 7(4)(b) of Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP. Groups should not bunch up inside either: gathering three or more vehicles on the roadway or in a tunnel costs 400,000 to 600,000 VND under Article 7(2)(d) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP). Our guide to riding in a group explains how groups travel safely.

Rule 3: no overtaking, no U-turns, no reversing

Three more bans apply inside. Overtaking is banned by Article 14(6)(k), U-turns by Article 15(4) and reversing by Article 16(2). A U-turn in a tunnel costs 800,000 to 1,000,000 VND under Article 7(4)(d) of Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP, and the same applies under Article 7(4)(c) to overtaking where it is banned. If you missed your exit or took the wrong tunnel, ride through to the end and find a legal place to turn. Our guides to overtaking and U-turns have the details.

Breaking down in a tunnel

If your bike dies in a tunnel, Article 26(2) spells out what to do:

1. Get into an emergency bay. Move the vehicle to a place designated for emergency stops. 2. Secure the scene if you cannot. If the bike cannot be moved, switch on the hazard lights and place a warning sign or light at a safe distance behind it. 3. Report it. Inform the traffic police responsible for the route, or the tunnel operator, straight away.

On a motorbike that means pushing the bike as far to the side or into the bay as you can, not standing on the roadway yourself, and calling for help. The police can be reached on 113 (THƯ VIỆN PHÁP LUẬT). Do not let another rider tow you out or push you along with a foot: using a motorbike to tow or push another vehicle is banned by Article 33(3)(đ) of the law. Our guide on what to do if your rental bike breaks down covers the rest.

What breaking the rules costs

The main amounts under Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP at a glance:

  • No dipped headlights in a tunnel: 600,000 to 800,000 VND, Article 7(3)(h) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP).
  • Stopping or parking in a tunnel outside designated places: 800,000 to 1,000,000 VND, Article 7(4)(b) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP).
  • U-turn in a tunnel: 800,000 to 1,000,000 VND, Article 7(4)(d) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP).
  • Overtaking where it is banned: 800,000 to 1,000,000 VND, Article 7(4)(c) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP).
  • Three or more vehicles gathering in a tunnel: 400,000 to 600,000 VND, Article 7(2)(d) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP).

Practical tips

  • Sunglasses off, visor up. Riding into a tunnel with dark lenses leaves you nearly blind for the first seconds. Take sunglasses off before the entrance.
  • Leave more space. There is little room to swerve, so keep a longer gap to the vehicle ahead than usual.
  • Hold your line. Keep to the right in your lane and ride smoothly.
  • Brief your passenger. Tell them before the entrance that nobody stops inside, not even for a photo.
  • Know your route. If you know which exit follows the tunnel, you will not need to search or turn inside it.
  • Expect the glare. Daylight dazzles at the exit, so ease off a little until your eyes adjust.
  • Test the light first. A dead headlight is most dangerous in a tunnel, so switch it on and check it at pickup and before every long ride.

Frequently asked questions

Do these rules apply to city underpasses? Article 12(3)(đ) names underpasses and road tunnels as places where you must ride attentively and slow down. Whether a particular underpass counts as a road tunnel under Article 26 depends on how it is classified. If in doubt, switch the light on; it costs you nothing.

May I use the horn in a tunnel? Under Article 21(1) the horn is only for warning of danger or signalling that you intend to overtake. Since overtaking is banned in tunnels, only a warning of danger remains. Our guide to horn rules has more.

What if my headlight fails inside? Ride on carefully, with plenty of distance, to the end of the tunnel and stop only outside, somewhere safe; inside you may only stop for a breakdown, in the emergency bay (Article 26(2)). A bike without a working headlight should not be ridden at all: under Article 14(1)(c) of Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP that costs 400,000 to 600,000 VND. Have the shop repair it before you ride on.

In short

In a tunnel: dipped headlights on, no stopping, no overtaking, no U-turns and no reversing (Articles 14, 15, 16 and 26). If you break down, use the emergency bay, otherwise hazard lights and a warning sign, and inform the police or tunnel operator at once. Breaking the rules costs between 400,000 and 1,000,000 VND under Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP.
