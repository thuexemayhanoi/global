The Honda Smart Key on a Rental Scooter

How the Honda Smart Key works on rental scooters such as the Vision, LEAD and Air Blade, what the positions on the ignition knob mean and why you should call the shop if the fob stops working.

Many current rental scooters in Vietnam no longer have a traditional ignition key but a keyless Smart Key: Honda offers it on models including the Vision, one of the most common rental scooters (Honda Vietnam). For travellers who only know mechanical keys, that raises questions. How do you start it? How do you open the seat to refuel? And what happens when the fob stops responding? This guide explains the basics from Honda Vietnam's information and tells you what to clarify at pickup. For an overview of choosing a bike, see our topic page on choosing a rental bike.

How the system works

With a Smart Key you carry a small remote fob, which Honda calls the FOB. Instead of a keyhole, the scooter has an ignition knob. When the scooter detects the fob nearby, the knob can be turned. Honda lists positions on the knob including OFF, LOCK, SEAT/FUEL and ON (Honda Vietnam, Smart Key FAQ):

  • ON: ignition on; the engine can be started.
  • OFF: ignition off.
  • LOCK: steering lock; the handlebars are locked.
  • SEAT/FUEL: the position for opening the seat or the fuel cap.

Exactly how you press and turn the knob varies by model. Ask the shop to demonstrate it once at pickup and try it yourself before you ride off.

The buttons on the fob

Honda's fob has an on/off button and a button for finding the vehicle, in Vietnamese "xác định vị trí xe". Honda also explains how to switch the scooter's answer-back sound on and off; depending on the model this is done with the ignition knob or the starter button (Honda Vietnam). In a big scooter car park, the locate function is handy for finding yours among dozens of identical scooters.

Which models use it

In its Smart Key answers, Honda mentions models including the SH125i and SH150i, SH Mode, PCX, Air Blade, LEAD and Vision (Honda Vietnam). Not every version of these models has a Smart Key; older or simpler versions still come with a conventional key. At the shop you will see it at once: if the scooter has a knob without a keyhole and you are handed a small fob instead of a key, it is a Smart Key. Our guide to the Honda Wave, Vision, Air Blade and Lead explains which models shops commonly offer.

Starting, refuelling, parking

For everyday use, a few steps are enough:

1. Switch the fob on and keep it on you, for example in a pocket. 2. Turn the knob to ON, then start as the shop showed you; on many scooters a brake lever must be pulled. 3. To refuel, switch off the engine and turn the knob to SEAT/FUEL to open the fuel cap or the seat. 4. When parking, turn to OFF and then to LOCK so that the handlebars are locked. 5. Take the fob with you. Never leave it in the storage compartment under the seat or on the scooter.

Our guide on where to park a motorbike in Hanoi explains where to leave the scooter safely.

If the fob does not respond

If the scooter does not respond to the fob, check the simple things first: is the fob switched on, and do you really have it with you? As with any remote fob, a weak battery in the fob can also be the cause.

For vehicles with the three-button fob, Honda describes an emergency procedure: the seat is first opened with an emergency key, then the vehicle battery cover is removed, a test connector is unplugged and an emergency-mode connector from the toolkit is plugged in. A code from the vehicle's ID card is then entered by pressing the ignition knob at set intervals, everything is reassembled and the knob is turned to ON within 6 minutes (Honda Vietnam).

For renters this is usually not an option: the emergency key, ID card and connector belong with the owner, and you should not work on the electrics yourself. Call the rental shop instead. Our guide on what to do if your rental bike breaks down covers the other steps.

Common misunderstandings

  • The scooter does not respond even though I have the fob: often the fob is switched off. Press the on/off button and try again.
  • The seat will not open: switch off the engine and turn the knob to SEAT/FUEL instead of pulling at the seat.
  • The scooter beeps when I lock it: that is usually the system's answer-back sound, which according to Honda can be switched on and off (Honda Vietnam).
  • The handlebars are locked: the knob is still at LOCK; turn it back to OFF and then to ON.

What to clarify at pickup

  • Demonstration: ask to be shown starting, the steering lock, the seat and the fuel cap.
  • Replacement: ask what the shop does if the fob stops working or is lost, and whether there is an emergency number.
  • Liability: check what the contract says about losing the fob; Smart Key systems cost more to replace than a simple key. Our guide to rental contract cancellation and changes covers the basics of the contract.
  • Deposit: make sure it is recorded that you returned the fob; our guide to rental deposits explains what to watch for.

Frequently asked questions

Can someone ride off with my scooter while the fob is in my pocket? If someone stands right next to the scooter while your fob is in your pocket nearby, the system may detect it. So always turn the knob to LOCK when you park, switch the fob off during longer stops and park in attended car parks.

May I give the fob to a travel companion? Only if that person is allowed to ride the scooter and the rental contract permits it. The law forbids handing a motor vehicle to someone who does not meet the legal conditions to ride it, for example a person without the right licence (Article 9(7) of Law 36/2024/QH15).

Does the Smart Key protect against theft? It makes theft harder but replaces neither the steering lock nor an attended car park. Use the LOCK position every time you park.

In short

The Honda Smart Key works with a remote fob and an ignition knob with the positions ON, OFF, LOCK and SEAT/FUEL; the fob has an on/off button and a locate button (Honda Vietnam). Ask for a demonstration at pickup, always take the fob with you and call the shop when there is a problem rather than trying the emergency procedure yourself.
