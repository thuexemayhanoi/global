Taking a Motorbike on a Ferry or Pontoon Bridge in Vietnam

How to take a rental motorbike on a river ferry or across a pontoon bridge in Vietnam: queuing, who boards first, why passengers walk on, the order for leaving and what the law bans.

On a motorbike trip through Vietnam, the road sometimes crosses water without a bridge: a vehicle ferry takes you over a river, or a floating pontoon bridge carries the road across. It is part of the adventure, but it raises questions. Who boards first? May your passenger stay on the bike? What applies on a narrow pontoon bridge? The Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety answers these in a dedicated article. This guide explains the rules and adds practical tips for the ramp and the crossing. For an overview of riding safely, see our topic page on safer riding and breakdown support.

Spotting a ferry landing

A ferry landing is announced by warning sign W.217 "Bến phà" (ferry landing) in the road sign regulation QCVN 41:2024/BGTVT. When you see it, slow down and expect a queue, people on foot and vehicles coming off the ferry towards you.

Queue without blocking the road

At a ferry landing or before a pontoon bridge, vehicles must queue in an orderly way without obstructing other traffic, under Article 23(1) of the law. For riders that means no squeezing past the queue to the front, and keeping the opposite lane clear so vehicles leaving the ferry can get off.

Who crosses first

Article 23(2) sets an order of priority: first priority vehicles such as fire engines and ambulances on duty, then vehicles carrying post, then vehicles carrying fresh food, and then public passenger transport. Within the same group, whoever arrives first crosses first. A rental motorbike belongs to none of the priority groups, so you take your turn in order of arrival and let the priority vehicles go ahead.

On the ferry: passengers get off

The rule that matters most if you ride two-up is in Article 23(3)(a): while boarding, during the crossing and when leaving on the other bank, nobody may sit on a vehicle except the rider. The only exceptions are children, pregnant women, older and frail people, people with disabilities and sick people. Your adult passenger therefore climbs off before the ramp, walks onto the ferry and gets back on only after you have ridden off on the far side. Our guide on how many people can ride one motorbike explains the passenger limits more generally.

Children fall under the exception and may stay on the bike, but hold them firmly, because ferries often jolt when docking. When your adult passenger gets back on, they should fasten their helmet before you move off: carrying a passenger without a helmet or with the strap open costs 400,000 to 600,000 VND under Article 7(2)(i) of Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP, with exceptions only for a few cases such as children under six and sick people being taken for emergency care.

The order for boarding and leaving

The sequence is regulated too. Under Article 23(3)(b), motor vehicles board first, followed by non-motorised vehicles and pedestrians. On arrival it is the other way round: pedestrians go ashore first, then vehicles leave, following the instructions of the person directing traffic. So do not ride off the moment the ramp drops; wait until the foot passengers are off and you are waved on.

Crossing a pontoon bridge

Pontoon bridges float on the water and are often narrow, with moving joints. Article 23(4) sets three rules:

  • Single file. Motor vehicles ride in one line in their direction of travel without obstructing oncoming traffic.
  • Slow traffic on the right. Non-motorised vehicles and pedestrians keep to the right and must not obstruct motor vehicles.
  • Follow directions. Everyone obeys the person directing traffic.

General bridge rules apply on top. At narrow bridges, Article 12(3)(đ) requires you to watch and slow down or stop. Overtaking on a narrow, single-lane bridge is banned by Article 14(6)(b), and stopping or parking on bridges is banned by Article 18(4)(c), except where traffic control allows it.

What breaking the rules costs

Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP sets these amounts for motorbike riders, among others:

  • Stopping or parking on a bridge: 600,000 to 800,000 VND under Article 7(3)(c) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP).
  • Overtaking where it is banned: 800,000 to 1,000,000 VND under Article 7(4)(c) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP).
  • Ignoring the signals or instructions of a person directing or controlling traffic: 4,000,000 to 6,000,000 VND under Article 7(7)(d), plus 4 points under Article 7(13)(b) (Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP).

Practical tips

  • Approach the ramp slowly. Ramps and joints can be wet, slippery or uneven. Ride at walking pace with both hands on the bars and your fingers ready on the brakes.
  • Hold the bike steady on board. Keep it secure so it does not roll or topple when the ferry docks. Switch the engine off once you are in place, keep the key with you and start up again only when it is your turn to leave.
  • Mind your footing. Decks and ramps are often wet; wear shoes with grip rather than flip-flops, especially if you have to push the bike a few metres.
  • Ask about the timetable. Ferry frequency varies widely. Ask locally or at your accommodation rather than relying on a set time, especially if you want to arrive before dark.
  • Watch the weather. Floods or heavy rain can disrupt services; our guide to riding in heavy rain helps with planning.
  • Check the bike first. Brakes and lights should work before you ride onto a ramp.

Frequently asked questions

May I overtake a slower bike on a pontoon bridge? No. Motor vehicles ride in single file there under Article 23(4)(a), and overtaking on narrow, single-lane bridges is banned anyway under Article 14(6)(b). Stay well back from the bike in front.

What if I cannot understand the instructions at the landing? Wait for a clear signal and follow the vehicles ahead. Do not move while foot passengers are still leaving. If in doubt, point at the ramp and wait for a nod.

In short

Queue without blocking the road and let priority vehicles go first (Article 23). On the ferry only the rider stays on the bike while the passenger walks; vehicles board first and leave last, as directed by the crew. On a pontoon bridge, ride in single file, do not overtake and do not stop. Stopping on a bridge costs 600,000 to 800,000 VND under Decree 168/2024/NĐ-CP.
