About this guide

About Vietnam Two-Wheel Guide

Vietnam Two-Wheel Guide is a multilingual information website about choosing, checking and responsibly using rental motorbikes and scooters in Hanoi and Vietnam. It provides practical checklists rather than a vehicle reservation service.

What readers can find here

Our current guides explain questions to ask before renting, how to inspect a vehicle and which contract terms deserve attention. The website is being developed across ten languages, with separate content structures for each audience. Planned categories may be visible before their articles are ready; a category label is not a guarantee that a guide has been published.

Editorial approach

We aim to separate confirmed facts from general suggestions, avoid unverified prices or availability, and encourage checking current driving regulations with authoritative sources. Articles are informational: they do not establish an insurance policy, guarantee legal driving eligibility or replace professional advice.

How guides are written and checked

Guides are drafted with AI assistance, natively in each of the ten languages, and every guide says so at its end. A guide is published only after automated checks: depth and structure, duplicate and overlapping topics, the page language, statements about the law or safety cite an official source such as a Vietnamese government portal or an official statute database, and every paragraph that names a price cites a dated source. These checks do not replace human editing: unless a guide states otherwise, no human editor has reviewed it.

Sources, dates and corrections

Each guide lists its references with the date they were checked, and its publication date is recorded in the page data and the sitemap. Laws, fines and prices change, so confirm anything important with the official source or the rental provider before you act on it. If you find an error or an outdated source, write to the editorial contact below with the page address and, if possible, a source; confirmed errors are corrected.

Contact the editorial team

For factual corrections, source suggestions and website enquiries, please visit our contact page or write to nguyentuantu8x@gmail.com. We do not publish a physical office address or telephone number for this editorial website.
